Add a new module inside the Linguist Portal left sidebar called:

LINGUIST STUDIO

This must be a real product module, not a placeholder page.

Goal:
Linguist Studio should allow linguists to use our platform as their own translation management system inside the Linguist Portal. It should let them manage their own clients, create jobs for those clients, attach localization assets, use AI drafting with integrated LLMs if desired, and work through the translation editor — similar in power to the customer portal, but as a separate linguist-owned module with its own logic and experience.

1. CORE PRODUCT MODEL
==================================================

Linguist Studio is a premium workspace area inside the Linguist Portal where a linguist can:

- create and manage their own private clients
- create translation jobs for those clients
- upload source files
- open jobs in the translation editor
- manage translation memories, glossaries, and style guides
- link localization assets to specific clients
- choose whether a job should use AI draft translation or be fully human
- revise AI-generated drafts inside the editor
- operate like a lightweight TMS inside the linguist portal

Important positioning:
- the linguist owns their client relationships
- client data is private to the linguist
- the linguist keeps their revenue
- the platform takes no cuts and no commission from the linguist’s client work
- the value is access to world-class tech with very low friction and affordable cost
