PLATFORM CONTEXT
==================================================

This SaaS is a modern translation operations platform where businesses can manage multilingual content using:
- AI / LLMs
- in-house linguists
- external linguists / vendors

- structured workflows for project creation, assignment, delivery, review, revision, notifications, approvals, and reporting

The platform may support:
- direct clients
- enterprise workspaces
- agencies managing their own clients
- sub-workspaces / client workspaces under an agency
