BUSINESS OVERVIEW (must include)
- Describe the platform: AI first draft + human linguist review/translation in one workflow.
- Explain the credit system and plans:
  - Free: BYOK + 100 monthly credits, max 5 docs/month, 1 TM, 100 glossary terms.
  - Professional: $49/month, 1000 monthly credits, unlimited docs/TMs/glossary.
  - Credit conversions:
    - AI translate: 1 credit = 5 words (ceil(words/5))
    - Human revision: 1 credit = 2 words (ceil(words/2))
    - Human translation: 2 credits = 1 word (words*2)
  - Internal linguists rule:
    - If job uses INTERNAL linguists and workflow is HUMAN_REVISION or HUMAN_TRANSLATION, human credits charged = 0.
- Explain linguist sourcing options:
  - System pool linguists
  - Preferred linguists
  - Internal linguists (client invited via email)
